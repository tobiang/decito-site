Delete account

This is a convenience translation. Only the German version is legally binding: Konto löschen.

In the app

The direct route: Settings → Delete account. The deletion takes effect immediately and does not require checking back with us.

Lists that belong to you are deleted along with it — shared ones too, for all members. The app points this out beforehand; if you want to keep a shared list for the others, first transfer its ownership in the list settings.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, an email to hello@splash.green with the subject “Delete account” is enough. State the email address you signed in with; we will reply to exactly that address for confirmation and then delete the account.

Without an email address

An anonymous account has no email address by which we could recognize you. Instead, it carries an ID: a long string of characters that the app shows under “Settings → Account” and copies to the clipboard when tapped. Send this ID to hello@splash.green, and the associated account will be deleted.

This ID is the only thing by which an anonymous account can be recognized — Art. 11(2) GDPR describes exactly this case. Keep it accordingly: whoever has it can request the deletion. Without it, we cannot attribute an anonymous account to anyone, however much we might want to.

If you have neither the app nor the ID, it takes care of itself: an anonymous account that has not been used for two years is deleted together with its contents. A shared list is not deleted along with it but passes to its longest-standing member; that member sees a notice about it the next time they open the app.

What is deleted

The account, your profile including display name and email address, your lists including entries and uploaded images, as well as your ratings and notes. Information about how you used the app is deleted or — where it belongs to a shared list that continues to exist — separated from any link to you.

Contributions you made to a shared list — entries together with their images — remain in that list if it belongs to another member or has been transferred. These contributions then belong to the list, no longer to you.

Deleting only individual data

You do not have to give up your whole account to get rid of something. You delete individual entries, images, tags and entire lists yourself in the app — permanently and immediately, without checking back with us and without a trash folder from which they could reappear later.

If you cannot get to something you want deleted, an email to hello@splash.green is enough. Say which content it concerns, and state the email address of your account or — for an anonymous account — its ID. Without one or the other, we cannot attribute you to an account and therefore cannot delete anything.

What remains

If content of yours was ever reported or moderated, the report and the moderation log are kept and are automatically deleted after three years. Without this exception, a measure could be erased without a trace by deleting one’s own account. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
