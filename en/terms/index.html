Reporting content

Shared lists are accessible by invitation only; nothing in Decito is public. Anyone who can see a list can report any entry in it directly in the app — and any member can also delete an entry themselves.

If you are affected yourself and are not a member — for example, as the creator of an image —, an email to hello@splash.green is enough. For us to be able to act, the report must contain (Art. 16(2) DSA):

  • a statement of reasons why the content is unlawful;
  • information that allows it to be located — the list, the entry, the time or the image itself; there is no public address, because nothing is publicly posted;
  • your name and your email address;
  • a confirmation that the information is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge.

We review every report and remove or block access to whatever must be removed. The affected person receives a statement of reasons and information about their available means of redress (Art. 17 DSA). Until a report is made, we know nothing about a piece of content — that is the role that Section 10 DDG and Art. 6 DSA assign to a hosting service, and we do not monitor anything preemptively.
