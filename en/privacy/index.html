Privacy Policy

This is a convenience translation. Only the German version is legally binding: Datenschutzerklärung.

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Controller

Decito is developed and operated by a single individual. The controller within the meaning of the GDPR is Tobias Langen, Germany.
Email: hello@splash.green
There is no data protection officer; the requirements of Art. 37 GDPR are not met.

In short

Decito contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting libraries. There are no advertising IDs, no profiling across providers and no resale of data. What is processed is what you create in the app, and what operating it technically requires. An error report leaves your device only if you send it yourself — see Feedback and error reports.

This policy applies to the app and to this website. Where something applies to only one of the two, this is stated there.

This website

The pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you access them, your browser transmits technically necessary data, which GitHub stores in server logs — in particular your IP address, date and time, the requested address and browser and system identifiers. We have no access to this and do not evaluate any of it.

The website sets no cookies, embeds no third-party fonts, maps or videos, and loads nothing from any other server. For the transfer to the USA, GitHub relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses under Art. 46 GDPR.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — the legitimate interest in being able to serve the pages at all and keep them stable.

Account and sign-in

You can use Decito anonymously: in that case, an account without an email address is created that consists only of a random identifier. If you sign in via magic link, your email address is stored as well. You also choose a display name; the app asks for it once on first launch, because otherwise nobody in a shared list would know who a contribution came from. What you enter there is up to you — a first name is enough, as is a nickname; it does not have to be your name. The app also transmits your device’s time zone offset, so that “today” is your calendar day and not the server’s.

Optionally, you can give your profile a color and a symbol; the others see both next to your name in your shared lists. Equally optionally, you choose a federal state: it determines which public holidays and school holidays apply under “Allowed days” in your lists, and with it the app tells you on the home screen, in “Plans” and in the day notifications what kind of day it is. For these notifications, it stores the names of the upcoming days and your federal state on your device. If a public holiday applies there only in part of the state, you decide yourself whether it counts for you — only that choice is then stored, never your place of residence. The members of your lists see your federal state, because it determines when entries in them come back.

The app asks for nothing more: apart from the display name, no information is required, and without an email address Decito works fully, except for signing in on a second device.

There is no sign-in via third parties — Google, Apple, social networks. So no account with any such provider is touched, and none of them learns that you use Decito.

An anonymous account lives only on this device. Without an email address, there is no attribute by which anyone could prove they are entitled to it — neither you nor we. If the device is lost, the account is unreachable for everyone. That is why an anonymous account that has not been used for two years is deleted together with its contents. Every launch of the app resets this period, as soon as the app reaches the server in doing so — opening it is enough. For this purpose, for anonymous accounts we store when the app was last opened (to the nearest half day); for accounts with an email address, this is not recorded. The app reminds you in good time and several times beforehand, and consistently offers to add and confirm an email address; after that, the period no longer applies.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the features you request).

Content you create

Everything you record in a list is stored — currently, for example, entries, notes, tags, dates, ratings, polls and uploaded images. This content is visible to the members of the respective list — that is the purpose of the app. Anyone who shares a list with you sees the associated contributions and your display name.

What you write in a free text field or upload as an image is your decision. If you store information about other people there, you are processing it — and you answer for the rights to an uploaded image; the Terms of Use say what that means.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

How you use the app

We store when an entry was completed or reopened and how you responded to suggestions. Like the rest of your content, this data is held at Supabase (see Recipients). The app computes the recommendations and statistics from it on your device, and does so within your own lists: they are not combined with other services, not linked into a profile across lists and do not serve any advertising. In a shared list, your yes and no responses to an entry feed into the shared tally of opinion that the other members see — that is what they are for.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

No tracking across providers

Decito does not track you across other apps or websites. It reads no advertising identifier — neither the Advertising ID on Android nor the IDFA on iOS —, embeds no third-party measurement tools and passes nothing to advertising networks or data brokers. That is also why the app does not ask for permission to track on iOS (App Tracking Transparency): there is nothing it would need to ask for.

On your device

The app keeps a local copy of your data so that it can show something immediately after launch, along with settings such as sorting and display, and the sign-in session. Personal intentions such as food focus and mindful shopping also stay on the device and do not reach the server. For tag suggestions, a language model is downloaded once from the provider’s storage (see Recipients) and then run exclusively on the device. The suggestion feature can be turned off in the settings.

If you want to take text from a photo, the app uses your device’s text recognition — on Android, that of Google Play services (ML Kit), whose recognition model the operating system downloads from Google as needed. The recognition itself runs exclusively on the device, the photo does not leave it for this, and none of this happens without your action: the feature becomes active only when you invoke it.

To follow an invitation, you can scan the QR code on the inviting person’s device with the camera. The camera is opened only for this process and closed again afterwards. Recognizing the code is handled by your device’s built-in capabilities — on Android, Google Play services (ML Kit), which download the recognition model from Google as needed; on iOS, the system functions supplied by Apple, for which nothing is downloaded. In both cases, the analysis runs exclusively on the device, no image is stored and none is transmitted, and only the invitation ID contained in the code is used.

None of this serves analysis or re-identification; all of it is necessary for the feature you invoked, which is why it falls under the exception in Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG and does not require consent. When you uninstall the app, this local data is removed from the device — except for a device backup, if you have one.

Your operating system may include this local copy in your device’s backup — on Android in your Google account, on iOS in your iCloud. This backup is held in your own account with the respective provider and on its terms; we have no access to it. Whether it takes place is up to you in the system settings.

Entries with a link

If you share a link from another app to Decito, Decito accesses that address directly from your device to determine the title and preview image; for IMDb links, it additionally accesses IMDb’s suggestion interface. In doing so, the website accessed learns your IP address and the program’s identifier (user agent), just as with any access in a browser. No intermediate server is used, and we receive no data through this.

It does not stop at this one access: the preview image and site icon are stored as an address, not as a copy. Both are therefore fetched from the original website — on the device of every member who opens the list, not just on yours; after that, each device shows them from its local cache until that cache is cleared. With each retrieval, the website learns the IP address of the respective device. Without an entry with a link, none of this takes place.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Notifications

Reminders are generated by the device itself from data it already has. There is no push service, no device identifier for notifications and therefore no recipient either: nothing leaves the device, and you can revoke the permission at any time in the operating system.

No automated decisions

No automated decision within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place. The app’s recommendations order your own entries according to your own input — they do not evaluate any person, have no legal effect, and you can disregard any suggestion.

Reports and moderation

Decito stores content on behalf of the people who use it and is therefore a hosting service within the meaning of the Digital Services Act (DSA). You can report content using the reporting feature in the app (Art. 16 DSA); anyone who is not a member of the list — for example, the creator of an image — can reach us by email, see Reporting content. If something is removed or blocked as a result, the affected person receives a statement of reasons and information about their available means of redress (Art. 17 DSA). Shared lists are accessible only by invitation and thus do not disseminate anything to the public; the additional obligations for online platforms therefore do not apply.

We store the report, the reported content and the IDs of the reporting and the uploading person; for a report by email, additionally the name and address of the reporting person, which Art. 16(2) DSA requires for an actionable report. In addition, a moderation log is kept for every measure.

The report and the log are retained even after an account is deleted and contain the ID and the display name of the uploading person as it was at the time of the report; the ID of the reporting person is removed when that person’s account is deleted. Both are automatically deleted after three years — the report three years after it was received, the log three years after the measure. Without this exception, a measure could be erased without a trace by deleting one’s own account — and a statement of reasons that is challenged later could no longer be verified.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR; retention despite a request for erasure is based on Art. 17(3)(e) GDPR.

Feedback and error reports

In the app, you can write feedback to us and manually send an error that occurred as a report. None of this happens automatically: the app contains no crash-reporting library, and an error leaves your device only if you tap Send yourself.

With an entry, the following is stored: your text; for an error report, the error message and the technical trace (stack trace) together with the place in the app last opened; in addition, the app version, processor architecture, time zone, Android version, language setting, device model and the status of the notification permission — as well as your account ID.

You can see your entries again in the app, together with any response from us (under review, resolved or declined, with at most one note). You can mark any entry as resolved and delete it at any time; when your account is deleted, all your entries disappear. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in a working product, with the transmission always initiated by you.

Retention period

  • Account with an email address and its content: until you delete the account.
  • Anonymous account and its content: until you delete it, at the latest two years after last use.
  • Content in shared lists: contributions that other members continue to use may remain in their lists.
  • Reports about content: three years from receipt.
  • Feedback and error reports: until you delete the entry, at the latest when your account is deleted.
  • Moderation log: three years from the measure.
  • Local data on the device: until uninstallation or until you clear the app’s storage; the names of the upcoming days also as soon as you sign out.
  • Server logs of this website: according to GitHub’s retention periods, over which we have no influence.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself in the app under “Settings”. The account, profile and your content are removed — with the exception of the records described under Reports and moderation. Contributions in other people’s shared lists remain in those lists but lose any link to you; the same applies to completion and reaction data already recorded there.

Lists that belong to you are deleted along with it — shared ones too, for all members. The app points this out before the deletion; if you want to keep a shared list for the others, transfer its ownership beforehand in the list settings. An ongoing poll that would be affected by the deletion is ended in the process; the app tells you this beforehand.

If, on the other hand, the two-year period of an anonymous account expires, there is no one who could be asked. A shared list is then not deleted but passes to the member who has belonged to it the longest — the others’ contributions depend on it, and they did not agree to its disappearance. That member sees a notice the next time they open the app that the list has passed to them. Only lists in which there is no one else are deleted along with it.

It also works without the app: Delete account without the app describes the route by email.

Security

The connection between the app and the server is encrypted throughout (TLS); on the server, the data is encrypted at rest. Which row belongs to which account is decided by the database itself through access rules that apply to every query — not by the app. Anyone who is not a member of a list does not receive its content, even upon request.

Age

Decito is not directed at children. Anyone under the age of 16 should use the app only with the consent of their parents or legal guardians.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). For access and data portability, an email to hello@splash.green is enough; for data portability, you receive your data in a commonly used, machine-readable format (Art. 20 GDPR).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Insofar as data is processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — the server logs of this website and the moderation log —, you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

If you use Decito anonymously, we cannot identify you without additional information; in that case, Art. 15–20 GDPR apply only insofar as you provide the missing information (Art. 11(2) GDPR). That is the ID that the app shows under “Settings → Account” — give it to us, and your request can be answered.

You can also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the federal state of your habitual residence.

Changes

If the data the app processes changes, this policy is updated together with the change. The date at the top states which version is current.
