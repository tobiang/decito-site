Delete account

This is a convenience translation. Only the German version is legally binding: Konto löschen.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

In the app

The direct way: Account (the person icon at the top of the home screen) → Delete account; for an account without an email address the button reads Delete all data. Deletion happens immediately and does not need me; image files from lists deleted along with it are gone by the following day at the latest.

Lists you own that still have other members pass to the member who has belonged to them longest; only lists with nobody else in them are deleted along with your account. There the app shows you beforehand which lists pass on and which are deleted.

An account without an email address is also deleted when you use it to sign in to an existing account (“Sign in with an existing account” or “Sign in there”): afterwards no device could reach it any more. If it holds nothing yet, this happens without a question; otherwise the app first shows you what is deleted and which lists pass on. The two accounts are not merged.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, an email to hello@splash.green with the subject “Delete account” is enough. Give the email address you signed in with; I will get back to you for confirmation at exactly that address and delete afterwards.

Without an email address

An account without an email address gives me nothing by which I could recognize you. Instead, it carries a code: sixteen characters in four groups, which the app shows under Account as “This account’s code” and copies to the clipboard on a tap. Send this code to hello@splash.green, and the associated account will be deleted.

In the app, only you see the code. Keep it like a key: whoever has it can request deletion. It is the only thing by which an account without an email address can be recognized — Art. 11(2) GDPR describes exactly this case —, and without it I cannot match such an account to anyone, not even with the best will in the world. If your app still shows an “ID” instead of a code, update it first: the old ID is no longer enough, because it also reaches the members of your shared lists. If you only noted the ID and can no longer reach the app, write to me anyway; I will check whether the request can be matched safely in another way.

If you have neither the app nor the code, it takes care of itself: an account without an email address that has not been used for two years is deleted together with its contents. A shared list passes to its longest-standing member instead of being deleted; that member sees a notice about it the next time they open the app.

What is deleted

The account, your profile including display name and email address, every list with nobody else in it, including its entries and uploaded images, and your verdicts on entries (stars and remark), including in other people’s lists. Information about how you used the app is deleted or — where it belongs to a shared list that continues to exist — separated from any link to you.

What you added to a shared list — entries including their images — stays in that list and is still shown there, without your name. Whatever you don’t want to leave behind, photo or entry, delete yourself first.

Leaving a list

If you leave a shared list without deleting your account, your entries in it stay for the others. If you want to remove some, delete them before leaving — they then disappear for everyone.

Deleting only individual data

You don’t have to give up your whole account to get rid of something. What you created — entries with their images — you delete yourself in the app; in lists you own, also the others’ entries and the whole list. It is permanent and does not need me; there is no recycle bin. The image files of a deleted list are gone by the following day at the latest.

If there is something you want deleted but cannot reach, an email to hello@splash.green is enough. Say which content it concerns, and give your account’s email address or — for an account without an email address — its code. Without one or the other, I cannot match you to an account and therefore cannot delete anything.

What remains

If someone has reported content of yours, the report — with the title and image address of the reported entry — and the moderation log for it remain, even if you delete your account, and are deleted automatically after three years. Without this exception, a measure could be erased without trace by deleting one’s own account. The details are in the privacy policy.
