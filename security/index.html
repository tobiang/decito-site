Reporting a vulnerability

Decito is built by one person. If you have found a security problem, telling us first — and giving us time to fix it — is genuinely welcome, and this page says what you can expect in return.

How to report

Write to hello@splash.green with the subject “Security report”. Useful things to include: what you found, the steps to reproduce it, what an attacker could do with it, and anything that helps us tell a real finding from scanner noise. Rough notes beat no report — do not wait until you have written it up properly.

What you can expect

A human acknowledgement, usually within 5 working days, and an assessment — is it real, how bad, what happens next — usually within 14 days. If a fix is going to take longer than that, you will hear why and roughly when.

These are the honest numbers for a project maintained by one person in their own time, not the numbers a company with an on-call rota would give. If a deadline passes without a word, send a reminder; something has gone wrong at our end.

Good-faith research is authorised

Testing that stays inside this policy is done with our permission. That matters more in Germany than the phrase suggests: § 202a StGB punishes access that is unbefugt — unauthorised — and this page is the authorisation. Within the rules below:

We will not report you, and we will not bring civil claims against you over your research. If someone else raises a complaint about testing that followed this policy, we will say publicly that it was authorised.

What no operator can promise: we can only waive our own rights. We cannot authorise access to other people's accounts or data, we cannot bind our hosting providers, and a public prosecutor is not ours to speak for. Staying inside the rules below is what keeps all of that theoretical.

The rules

Use your own accounts. Decito lets you create an account in a tap, and a second one to test sharing against. Never test against an account you do not own.

Stop at proof. Once you can show a vulnerability exists, stop. Do not read further, do not enumerate, do not download, do not pivot. A screenshot of one record you were not supposed to see proves the point; a dump of the table does not prove it better.

If you reach someone's personal data, that is the end of the test. Tell us what you saw and delete your copy. Keeping it would make you a controller of data you have no basis to hold, and would turn our incident into yours as well.

Do not degrade the service. No denial of service, no load or stress testing, no automated scanning that generates real traffic, no spam through any form the app can send.

Attack the software, not the people. No phishing, no social engineering of us or of anyone using Decito, no physical access attempts.

In scope

The Decito apps for Android and iOS, this website, and the backend they talk to — the API, the database's access rules, and the stored images. Both apps speak to the same backend, so a finding in one is usually a finding in both; say which you tested on, and we will check the other. Authorisation flaws are the most interesting class here: Decito's lists are shared, so “can one member see or change something they should not” is the question the whole permission model exists to answer.

Out of scope

Findings in the services Decito is built on rather than in Decito — Supabase, Google Play, the App Store, the e-mail provider — belong to their own programmes; report them there. Also out of scope: missing hardening headers with no exploit behind them, results from automated scanners that nobody has verified by hand, reports that an old version of a library is old without a path to exploiting it, and anything that needs a rooted or jailbroken device, or an attacker who already controls the phone.

Publishing

Please give us 90 days from your report before making it public. If a fix ships sooner, you are free to publish as soon as it is out. If we go quiet, or if the 90 days pass without a fix, you are free to publish then too — a disclosure deadline is what stops coordinated disclosure from becoming indefinite silence.

Tell us if you would like to be credited, and how you want to be named. There is no bounty: Decito is free, has no revenue, and promising money we do not have would be worse than saying so plainly.
